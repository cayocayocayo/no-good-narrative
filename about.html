no-good narrative

no-good narrative is a DIY label for political technology: tools, research and events making sense at the intersection of politics and mass-communications technology.

Released openly and as work in progress, interested in new ways forward.

I'm Rhi Rowlands, a communications professional, musician (as Sharks on Vinyl) and researcher based in Stoke Newington, and a 2026–27 fellow at Newspeak House. I'm interested in propaganda, hauntology, liberation politics and how software can make complex topics legible.
